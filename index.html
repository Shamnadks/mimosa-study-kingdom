📝 The KUHS Exam: know your enemy

Checked against your official model papers and real KUHS papers from May 2022, Nov 2022, Jul 2023 and Dec 2023. Every theory paper follows the same pattern.

📜

Essays · 2 × 10 = 20

~25 min each · 2½–3 pages · intro → headings → diagram/table → conclusion.
📝

Short notes · 7 × 5 = 35

~12 min each · 1–1½ pages · definition + 5–8 points + a small diagram/table.
⚡

Answer briefly · 10 × 2 = 20

~3½ min each · definition + 2–4 key points or one example.
Essays · 50 min
Short notes · 85 min
Brief · 35 min
✔ 10
⚠️ There is NO choiceKUHS says "Answer all questions". Every one of the 19 questions must be attempted, so cover the whole syllabus instead of skipping units.
⭐ Marks love structureHeadings, underlined keywords, labelled diagrams, comparison tables. Each unit page shows you exactly how to lay out the answer.

Each theory paper is a 75-mark university exam (3 hours). Internal assessment marks are added separately by the college.
