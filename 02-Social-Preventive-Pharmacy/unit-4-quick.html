4.1 Mother & Child 👩‍🍼

Queen Elinor's pregnancy kit: a cash bonus, a pink card, and a monthly village party. That's literally the whole topic. 😄

💰

PMMVY 2017

  • Maternity cash benefit, by Ministry of Women & Child Development
  • For pregnant/lactating women ≥19 yrs, first live birth
  • Objectives: safe delivery, good nutrition, exclusive breastfeeding 6 months, partial wage-loss compensation
  • Book timeline: JSY 2005 → IGMSY → renamed PMMVY 2017
🏥

RCH Programme

  • Phase I 1997: drug kits to PHCs/FRUs, 24-hr delivery at PHC/CHC, referral transport, immunisation + ORS, Vit-A, IMCI, blood storage at FRU
  • Phase II 2005 with NRHM: cut maternal & infant deaths, adolescent health, RTI/STI control
🩷

MCP Card (Mother & Child Protection Card)

  • One card tracking mother's pregnancy + child's growth & vaccines
  • Targets: pregnant women, lactating women, children <3 yrs, JSY/IGMSY/JSSK/NRC beneficiaries
  • Users: family, village groups (VHSNC), AWW · ANM · ASHA, ICDS supervisors
🎪

VHND (Village Health & Nutrition Day)

  • Once a month at the Anganwadi centre
  • ANM + ASHA + AWW give ANC check-ups, vaccines, nutrition advice
  • Outcome: more awareness, better coverage, community takes part

PMMVY money flow (draw this!)

🧠 4.1 in one line"P-R-M-V" = Pay her (PMMVY) · Reach the hospital (RCH) · Mark the card (MCP) · Village party monthly (VHND).

4.3 National Tobacco Control 🚭

Cruella de Vil + cigarette holder = the poster villain. COTPA is the law that would ruin her whole look. 💨❌

NTCP 2007–08

Launched by MoHFW in the 11th Five Year Plan. India is a party to WHO's FCTC (Framework Convention on Tobacco Control). Tobacco = Nicotiana tabacum, family Solanaceae.

COTPA 2003

Cigarettes & Other Tobacco Products Act. Covers cigarettes, bidis, gutka, khaini, snuff… whole of India.
SectionRuleRemember it as
Sec 4No smoking in public places (hospitals, railway stations, offices, restaurants…)🚬 Smoke
Sec 5No advertisement, promotion or sponsorship📺 Show
Sec 6aNo sale to anyone under 18🧒 Sell
Sec 6bNo sale within 100 yards of educational institutions🏫 School
Sec 7Pictorial health warning on every pack☠️ Sign
🧠 4-5-6-7"Smoke, Show, Sell, Sign" — four sections, four S's. Gutka itself is banned under the Food Safety Regulations, 2011.

GATS (Global Adult Tobacco Survey): nationally representative survey that monitors adult tobacco use with a standard global protocol.

4.5 Health Care of the Elderly (NPHCE) 👴🎈

Carl from Up tied balloons to his house. NPHCE ties geriatric care to every level of the health system, minus the balloons. 🎈

Key facts

2010 by MoHFW, 11th plan, ₹288 crore approved (incl. 20% State share). For senior citizens >60 years. Covers preventive, curative and rehabilitative care.

Why?

India's 60+ population has tripled in 50 years; we're heading to the world's 2nd-largest elderly population.

4.7 Role of WHO in India's Programmes 🧞

The Genie doesn't rule Agrabah; he grants help when asked. WHO is the same: it advises and supports, India runs the programmes. 🪔

Quick facts

Founded 1948 (7 April = World Health Day). Its constitution lists 22 functions; the first is "directing & coordinating authority on international health work". South-East Asia regional office (SEARO) is in New Delhi. Works through a Country Cooperation Strategy (book: 2019–23).

What WHO does for India

🧪 technical guidance & standards · 💉 polio & immunisation surveillance · 🫁 TB, malaria, HIV support · 📊 data & disease surveillance · 🚨 outbreak/emergency help · 🎓 training health workers
