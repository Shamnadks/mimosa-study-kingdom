Exam Day & Between Papers

The ball has begun. Here is exactly what to do on each exam day and on the gap days between papers.

🌅 Exam morning

  • Light breakfast + water. No new topics!
  • Skim the subject's cheat sheets & Top-12 only (30–40 min).
  • Reach the hall 30 minutes early. Hall ticket + ID in a clear pouch.
  • Kit: 3 blue/black pens, pencil, eraser, scale, sharpener, watch, water bottle.

📝 In the hall

  • Read the whole paper first (3 min). Tick what you know best.
  • Write question numbers clearly in the margin; keep all parts of a question together.
  • Follow the time bar: Essays ~25 min each → Short notes ~12 min each → Briefs ~3.5 min each.
  • Headings, underlined keywords, a diagram/table in every long answer.
  • Never leave a blank: write the definition + 3 points for anything you half-know.
  • Last 10 minutes: check numbering, underline keywords.

🔁 Gap days between papers

  • Gap day 1: that subject's PYQ Bank Top-12 + model answers of its top 4 essays.
  • Gap day 2 (if any): all 5 cheat sheets + diagrams + one Brief Blitz.
  • Night before: cheat sheets + flashcards only, then sleep by 10 pm.

💖 After each paper

  • Don't discuss answers. That paper is done!
  • Eat, rest for an hour, then start the next subject's gap-day plan.
  • Collect your exam pin below ✔️
