1 Exam Pattern & Time Plan

KUHS 2017 scheme · QP Code 829006 · 3 hours · 75 marks · answer ALL questions (no choice) · "Draw diagrams wherever necessary".

📖

~2.5–3 pages each · ~25 min each · headings + diagram/table + conclusion.
✏️

Short notes: 7 × 5 = 35

~1–1.5 pages each · ~12 min each · definition + 4–6 points + 1 diagram or table.
⚡

~3–5 lines each · ~3.5 min each · definition + 2–4 bullets/examples.

⏱️ Your 180-minute plan

Essays 2 × 25 = 50 min
Short notes 7 × 12 = 84 min
Brief 10 × 3.5 = 35 min
Rev 10

Total 179 min. Suggested order: read the whole paper (1 min) → brief answers first (quick confidence and safe marks) → essays → short notes → final 10 min to re-read, underline keywords and finish labels on diagrams. Never leave a question blank: even 3 correct lines earn marks.

How to write each type

📖 Essay (10 m)

  1. Definition / introduction (3–4 lines)
  2. Classification or table (e.g. types of sunscreens, hair dyes)
  3. Main body under headings: principle, ingredients, mechanism, method
  4. One big labelled diagram (hair, skin, sebumeter) or a formula table with functions
  5. Advantages / limitations / applications
  6. Conclusion (2 lines)

✏️ Short note (5 m)

  1. Definition (1 mark)
  2. 4–6 key points under 2–3 headings
  3. One small diagram, flow chart or table
  4. One line on applications or a conclusion

For "formulation" questions, ALWAYS give a formula table (ingredient · % · function) + method of preparation.

⚡ Brief (2 m)

  • Crisp definition (1 mark)
  • 2–4 examples or bullet points (1 mark)
  • "Give examples" → write at least 4
  • "Formula" → a small 4–6 row table with functions
