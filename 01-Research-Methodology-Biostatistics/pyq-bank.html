How to write each type for full marks

Essay (10 m)

  • Introduction/definition (1–1.5 m)
  • Main body under numbered headings (5–6 m)
  • At least one diagram, flowchart or table (1–2 m)
  • Examples from pharmacy
  • Conclusion (½–1 m)
  • Follow the mark split if printed (e.g., 1+2+2+5)

Short note (5 m)

  • Underlined definition
  • 5–8 crisp points or a comparison table
  • One labelled diagram or worked example
  • Numericals: formula → table → substitution → answer with unit → one-line interpretation

Brief (2 m)

  • One-line definition with keywords
  • 2–4 bullet points or a formula
  • One example
  • Never more than half a page

6 Exam-Hall Tips for This Paper

✏️ Diagrams & tables worth drawing

  • Research process flowchart (U1)
  • Primary vs secondary data table; histogram, bar, pie, frequency polygon (U2)
  • Study-design arrows: cohort (exposure → outcome, forward) and case-control (outcome → exposure, backward); 2×2 table for RR/OR (U3)
  • Normal curve with 68–95–99.7; skewness curves; sampling dot diagram; CONSORT mini flow (U4)
  • Critical-region curve; Type I/II 2×2 table; parametric ⇄ non-parametric twin chart; thesis sandwich (U5)

🧮 Numericals

  • Always: formula → table (x, f, cf, fx…) → substitution → answer with unit → one-line interpretation
  • Check inclusive classes (0–10, 11–20): convert to boundaries (10.5–20.5) before median/mode
  • Say whether SD uses n or n − 1
  • For tests: state H₀, α, df, critical value, decision
  • Graph from a table: title, labelled axes with units, scale, legend, neat bars (histogram bars touch, bar-diagram bars don't)

🔑 Keywords examiners look for

  • "systematic", "objective", "replicable" (research)
  • "representative", "random", "known probability", "sampling frame" (sampling)
  • "H₀", "α = 0.05", "critical region", "df", "p < 0.05", "power = 1 − β"
  • "distribution-free", "ranks", "median" (non-parametric)
  • "IMRaD", "ICMJE", "COPE", "FFP", "UGC 2018" (writing & ethics)

🧭 Strategy

  • All questions are compulsory: never leave a brief blank; a definition alone earns 1 mark
  • Underline definitions; use headings and bullets
  • Use pharmacy examples (drug vs placebo, tablets, BE studies)
  • Keep 10 minutes to number answers correctly and add missed diagrams
