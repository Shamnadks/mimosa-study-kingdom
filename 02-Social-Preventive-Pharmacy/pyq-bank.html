How to write each type in this theory-heavy subject

📕 Essay (10 m)

  1. Definition / introduction (2–3 lines).
  2. 6–8 underlined headings using a template (below).
  3. One table + one diagram — they carry ~2 marks.
  4. One current figure or 🆕 update (NFHS-6, SRS, renamed programme).
  5. 2-line conclusion.

📒 Short note (5 m)

  1. Definition with launch year / key fact.
  2. 5–8 numbered points under 2–3 headings.
  3. One small diagram, flow or table.
  4. One-line pharmacist role or outcome.

📗 Brief (2 m)

  1. Exact definition (1 mark).
  2. 2–4 bullets: examples, objectives, year (1 mark).
  3. No long intros — keep to 4–5 lines.

6 🎒 Exam-Hall Tips

✍️ Writing

  • Write the question number clearly in the margin; answer all parts of a question together.
  • Underline headings and keywords; use numbered points, not paragraphs.
  • Start every programme with its launch year and every disease with its agent.
  • Add one 🆕 update (NTEP, NCVBDC, Ayushman Arogya Mandir, NFHS-6) — it shows you are current.
  • End essays with a 2-line conclusion and a pharmacist role.

⏱️ Time

  • Watch check-points: 1 h 15 min → essays done + 1 short; 2 h 25 min → all shorts done.
  • If stuck, leave space and move on — come back in the last 10 minutes.
  • Never spend more than 4 min on a 2-marker.
